# Bush Bonanza audio sources

The two natural bird recordings used by Bush Bonanza are public-domain files from Wikimedia Commons. Both were trimmed, converted to mono MP3, level-normalised and faded for short in-game cues.

- `kookaburra-call.mp3`: excerpt from “LaughingKookaburra.ogg” by Kuco. Public domain. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:LaughingKookaburra.ogg
- `whipbird-call.mp3`: excerpt from “Eastern Whipbird.ogg” by Wardie44, recorded in the Illawarra Range near Wollongong. Public domain. https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eastern_Whipbird.ogg

The reel pull, spin, reel-stop, win and count-up sounds are generated locally with the Web Audio API. They require no network connection or third-party files.
